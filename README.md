## **Overview**

This **portfolio website** is built using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, with a strong emphasis on UI polish and real-world responsiveness. It's ideal for showcasing your professional works, competencies, and personal story in a concise, attractive layout. Every section is crafted to be clear, engaging, and mobile-friendly.

---

## **Features**

- **Fully Responsive:** Looks sharp on desktops, tablets, and mobiles.
- **Modern UI/UX:** Clean design, vivid visuals, and intuitive navigation.
- **Dedicated Sections:**
  - Home & Hero welcome
  - About Me
  - Skills, Services, or Experiences
  - Portfolio/Gallery of projects
  - Contact Form
  - Footer with social/contact info
- **Smooth Navigation:** Easy and quick jumps between sections via navbar or scroll.
- **Interactive Elements:** Button hovers, card highlights, and subtle animations.
- **Accessibility & Best Practices:** Organized HTML5, focused on usability.
- **Ready for Deployment:** Runs seamlessly via GitHub Pages or similar static hosts.

## **UI & Experience**

- **Bold Hero Section:** Eye-catching introduction and call to action.
- **Elegant Typography:** Professional, readable fonts and strategic use of weight/color.

## **Usage**

- **Update Profile:** Add your photo, intro, and contact/social links.
- **Add Projects:** Populate the Portfolio/Projects section with your own work.
- **Edit Skills/Experience:** Reflect your unique abilities and history.
- **Adjust Styles:** Modify `style.css` for bespoke brand colors and UI tweaks.

## **Getting Started**

1. **Clone or Download the Repository**
git clone https://github.com/your-username/Responsive_Personal_Portfolio.git
cd Responsive_Personal_Portfolio

3. **Run Locally**
- Double-click `index.html` or use a simple local server:
  ```
  npx serve .
  ```
- Visit `http://localhost:5000` (or the port shown).

3. **Customize Your Content**
- Edit `index.html` and `style.css` with your own info, projects, and images.
